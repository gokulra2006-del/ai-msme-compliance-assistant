# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion

## Users

MSME business owners managing their own compliance.

## Product Purpose

To provide an automated, AI-powered assistant that helps MSMEs track, understand, and fulfill their regulatory compliance requirements.

## Positioning

Differentiates by providing AI-driven suggestions and step-by-step resolution for compliance issues, rather than simply flagging gaps.

## Operating Context

Used primarily via a web dashboard by owners needing a clear overview of compliance status, upcoming deadlines, and actionable insights to resolve gaps.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Must be highly accessible and responsive.

## Brand Commitments

Indian compliance-focused identity. Professional navy, teal, and saffron color palette. Clear typography, subtle AI and glass effects. A trustworthy government and enterprise feel without looking like a dated government website.

## Evidence on Hand

None.

## Product Principles

- **Actionable over Informational**: Prioritize step-by-step resolution over just listing issues.
- **Trust and Authority**: Visuals must inspire trust, balancing enterprise professionalism with modern AI aesthetics.
- **Clarity and Density**: Present complex compliance information clearly, balancing data density with readability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Strong accessibility features required, ensuring high contrast and readable typography.
